# Penguin Snowball reference-style contract

Use this as a visual acceptance checklist, not as permission to copy source assets.

## Overall look

The target is a small Japanese indie/game-jam presentation:
- warm off-white / cream snow field;
- thick dark outlines;
- flat fills;
- intentionally simple, slightly awkward character drawing;
- almost no realistic rendering;
- minimal shading;
- lots of empty space;
- gameplay elements feel drawn on paper rather than assembled from a design system.

Avoid:
- polished vector mascot art;
- gradients on characters;
- blush, glossy highlights, realistic feathers;
- rounded SaaS/mobile-app cards;
- large drop shadows;
- modern dashboard layout;
- complex iconography.

## Penguin proportions

All penguins:
- compact SD body;
- body is one slightly asymmetric bean/egg mass;
- white belly is one large simple mass;
- small dot/line face;
- yellow/orange beak and feet;
- flippers are short leaf/bean shapes;
- outline width visually strong at gameplay scale.

Unit identity is silhouette-first:
- Small: tiniest, roundest, slightly baby-like.
- Gentoo: forward-leaning, faster silhouette.
- Chinstrap: taller/slimmer, restrained expression.
- Emperor: broad/heavy.
- King: largest, crown/crest cue, oversized throw.

## Title screen

Visual hierarchy:
1. game title;
2. group of penguins;
3. Start button;
4. small difficulty/time choices;
5. settings.

Do not add unit descriptions to the home screen.
Do not show stat cards.
Do not use two-column dashboard panels.

Composition should still read when viewed as a 390px-wide phone screenshot.

## Battle HUD

HUD must feel like part of the drawing:
- white/cream base;
- dark hand-drawn stroke;
- cyan/blue player accent;
- red enemy accent;
- yellow cheer/cost accents;
- circular unit faces;
- segmented cost meter;
- cloud-like or simple HP indicator.

Use minimal text.

## Motion

The charm comes from motion as much as drawing:
- walking has obvious waddle;
- snowball making is a visible kneading action;
- throw has anticipation and follow-through;
- king throw is dramatically larger;
- defeat can use tumble/white flag;
- cheer should visibly animate supporters and accelerate snowball making.

## Acceptance questions

Before declaring a screen done, answer yes to all:
- Does the screenshot look like a game rather than an app?
- Are the penguins clearly hand-drawn at first glance?
- Are gradients and glossy rendering absent from characters?
- Is the title screen mostly title, character art and start action?
- Are unit choices readable at phone size?
- Does battle UI leave most of the screen to the battlefield?
- Can a player understand placement and cheer without reading a paragraph?
